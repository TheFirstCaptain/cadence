# Cadence Vision

## Purpose

Cadence helps people build a sustainable rhythm in everyday life without turning wellness into a scorecard.

## Product promise

The app makes weekly intention-setting, daily follow-through, indulgence awareness, and reflection feel light enough to continue using. It supports the user without judging them.

## Principles

1. **Consistency over perfection.** A missed action is one moment, not a failed streak.
2. **Awareness without judgment.** Records help reveal patterns; they do not assign moral value.
3. **Planning without rigidity.** Intentions can adapt when life changes.
4. **Tracking without effort.** Frequent actions should take one tap or only a few seconds.
5. **Private by default.** Personal wellness information stays local unless a later, explicit decision changes that boundary.

## Initial audience

People who want gentle structure for everyday wellness but find conventional habit, diet, and productivity apps too demanding or punitive.

## Initial experience

- Plan a realistic week on Sunday.
- Check off today's commitments quickly.
- Log a custom indulgence through iCheated without shame.
- Review simple history to notice patterns over time.

## Measures of success

- The app is approachable after a disrupted week.
- Core daily actions are fast and understandable.
- History informs rather than scolds.
- The interface remains calm, accessible, and trustworthy.
