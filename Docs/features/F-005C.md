# Feature F-005C: Day History Detail

## Objective

Add a read-only Day detail that shows historically active Goals, their checkoffs, and individual iIndulged occurrences with stable recorded local times.

## User value

The user can inspect what happened on a particular day before deciding whether any record needs correction.

## Non-goals

- Adding or removing historical checkoffs.
- Deleting or restoring historical iIndulged occurrences.
- Adding iIndulged occurrences to past dates.
- Editing Goal definitions or category definitions.

## Acceptance notes

- Selecting an enabled day from Week view opens Day detail for that preserved civil date. Month follows the approved Month → Week → Day path.
- Day detail shows every Goal historically active on that date, including a clear checked/unchecked state.
- Removed or renamed historical Goal snapshots retain their historically correct names and ordering.
- Individual iIndulged occurrences show category and recorded local time ordered by instant, then stable occurrence ID as a tie-breaker.
- Recorded time formatting uses the stored time-zone identifier, with the stored UTC offset as a deterministic fallback if the identifier cannot be resolved later.
- Today is inspectable; future dates remain visible in the current Week but are disabled and announced as not available yet.
- Recorded times include the stored zone abbreviation, or an explicit `UTC±HH:MM` qualifier when fixed-offset fallback is required.
- Archived categories with occurrences on that day remain understandable.
- Empty, loading, ready, and recoverable failure states use calm neutral language.
- Returning preserves the selected Week or Month context.

## Clarification notes

- This slice makes F-005A day distribution interactive but remains strictly read-only.
- F-005D adds correction controls after the detail, identity, and accessibility contract is stable.
- Day detail uses push navigation within History's existing `NavigationStack`, preserving the selected Week or Month context in the navigation path.
- A day with no historically active Goals says “No Goals were planned for this day.” Goals remain visible as unchecked when they were planned but have no checkoff; an otherwise empty occurrence section says “No iIndulged entries were recorded.”

## Workflow

- Tier: 3.
- Rationale: Day detail reads personal historical data across activation, archived identity, and time-zone boundaries even though it does not mutate records.
