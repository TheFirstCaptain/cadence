# Feature F-003C: iIndulged Quick Logging

## Objective

Add the five built-in iIndulged counters to My Day with immediate, timestamped plus and most-recent-minus behavior.

## User value

The user can notice indulgence patterns with a fast, playful interaction that treats occasional enjoyment as normal and makes corrections easy.

## Non-goals

- Adding custom indulgence categories.
- Recording servings, quantities, units, calories, or moral scores.
- Adding past-dated occurrences.
- Building History summaries or recent-entry management.

## Acceptance notes

- iIndulged appears below Goals and initially lists Junk Food, Unhealthy Snacks, Candy, Alcohol, and Desserts in that order.
- Each row shows a monochrome SF Symbol, visible category name, minus control, today's count, and plus control.
- One plus tap records one occurrence with its precise instant and captured local-date/time-zone context.
- One minus tap removes the most recent occurrence for that category on the current local day.
- Counts update immediately and never fall below zero.
- Prior-day occurrences remain unchanged and available to History.
- Relaunch restores today's counts.
- Failed plus or minus mutations restore a coherent persisted presentation state and show calm guidance without exposing storage errors.
- Copy and feedback remain playful, brief, observational, and nonjudgmental.
- VoiceOver announces category counts and changes; controls have explicit labels and actions; Reduce Motion removes nonessential emphasis animation.
