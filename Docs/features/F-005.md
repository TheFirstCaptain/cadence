# Feature F-005: History

## Objective

Provide simple Week and Month views that show Goal progress and iIndulged activity without percentages, combined scores, or judgment, with drill-down and correction of personal records.

## User value

The user can understand how a week or month has gone, inspect what happened on a particular day, and correct inaccurate records while deciding for themselves what the patterns mean.

## Non-goals

- Creating a combined Cadence, wellness, or compliance score.
- Showing percentages, punitive streaks, forecasts, or better/worse judgments.
- Comparing the current period automatically with a prior period.
- Adding new iIndulged occurrences to past dates.
- Adding charts, exports, sharing, or cloud analytics.
- Creating the Xcode project before F-002 is approved.

## Acceptance notes

- History opens to the current Monday-through-Sunday week.
- A Week/Month control changes the time scale.
- Prior weeks and months are available without an artificial limit; future periods are unavailable.
- A Today action returns to the current period.
- Week view shows each Goal as a plain completed-of-target count such as 5 of 7 and shows daily checkoff placement.
- Week view shows every iIndulged category and its count, including zero counts, with daily distribution but no automatic comparison.
- Tapping a day opens Day detail.
- Month view represents one calendar month.
- Calendar-month iIndulged totals include only occurrences whose preserved local date falls in that month.
- Monthly Goal presentation uses compact full Monday-through-Sunday week rows so a weekly target is not split at a month boundary.
- Tapping a week opens its Week view.
- Day detail shows Goals and individual iIndulged occurrences with recorded local times.
- A past Goal checkoff may be added or removed only when the Goal was active on that date.
- An individual past iIndulged occurrence may be removed through swipe or an accessible action.
- Removing a past occurrence does not require confirmation and offers a temporary Undo action.
- Historical corrections immediately update affected Week and Month summaries.
- A Goal denominator includes only dates on which the Goal was active.
- Goals and iIndulged remain separate and are never combined into one score.
- Historical records remain anchored to the original local date and time-zone context in which they were created; travel does not regroup the past.

## Clarification notes

- The user selected the current week as the default.
- Goal results use counts rather than percentages.
- Automatic previous-period comparisons are excluded from V1.
- Tapping a day opens detailed records.
- Goal denominators include only active dates.
- Month means a normal calendar month rather than a rolling 30-day period.
- Monthly Goal presentation retains complete weekly rows across month boundaries.
- Zero-count iIndulged categories remain visible.
- Past Goal checkoffs and individual iIndulged occurrences may be corrected.
- Prior navigation is unlimited, future navigation is unavailable, and Today returns to the current period.
- Historical records keep their original local-date assignment after a time-zone change.

## Validation plan

- Unit-test current/prior period navigation and future-period guards.
- Unit-test Monday-through-Sunday Week aggregation and calendar Month aggregation.
- Test weeks crossing month and year boundaries without splitting weekly Goal targets.
- Verify Goal denominators use only active dates and reflect midweek add, change, and removal correctly.
- Verify all iIndulged categories remain visible at zero and monthly totals use occurrence local dates.
- Test day drill-down, valid historical Goal toggles, inactive-date guards, occurrence deletion, Undo, and derived-summary updates.
- Test local midnight, daylight-saving transitions, month/year rollover, and time-zone travel without regrouping existing records.
- Verify VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, focus order, accessible delete alternatives, Reduce Motion, and non-color-only meaning.
- Review all summaries and empty states for neutral, nonjudgmental language.
