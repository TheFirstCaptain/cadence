# Feature F-005A: Week History Foundation

## Objective

Establish repository-backed History navigation and a read-only Monday-through-Sunday Week view with plain Goal progress and neutral iIndulged distribution.

## User value

The user can open History and understand one week at a glance without scores, percentages, comparisons, or judgment.

## Non-goals

- Calendar Month presentation.
- Day-detail presentation or tappable day navigation before F-005C lands.
- Historical checkoff or occurrence correction.
- Charts, forecasts, prior-period comparisons, or combined scores.

## Acceptance notes

- History opens to the current local Monday-through-Sunday week.
- Prior weeks are available without an artificial limit; future weeks are unavailable.
- Today returns to the current week and is unavailable when already there.
- Week navigation and lifecycle refresh use captured local-day and time-zone identity with stale-read protection.
- Goal rows use completed-of-target counts and daily placement without percentages.
- Goal denominators count only dates on which that weekly Goal snapshot was historically active.
- Completed counts use distinct eligible dates and remain bounded by the effective target.
- iIndulged remains visually separate and shows daily distribution plus a weekly count.
- The category list includes the approved historical category set, including zero-count categories.
- Empty, loading, ready, and recoverable failure states remain calm and useful.
- A narrow composition-owned History revision can request a fresh atomic Week read after a later correction without coupling feature models or exposing persistence details.
- Meaning, selection, and completion are not conveyed by color alone.

## Clarification notes

- The parent F-005 record owns current-week default, unlimited prior navigation, future guards, Today behavior, plain counts, stable original local dates, and the prohibition on automatic comparisons.
- This slice owns the shared period-navigation, lifecycle-refresh, and neutral presentation contracts used by later History slices.
- Day selection remains visually inactive until F-005C provides a destination.
