# Feature F-002A: Domain and Calendar Foundation

## Objective

Create immutable, Sendable domain values and deterministic system dependencies for Cadence dates, weeks, identities, and Goal frequencies.

## User value

Weekly plans and personal records remain predictable across Sundays, daylight-saving changes, travel, and test environments.

## Non-goals

- Defining SwiftData models or repositories.
- Building product screens.
- Aggregating History or implementing planning workflows.

## Acceptance notes

- `LocalDay` represents a Gregorian civil date without relying on the device's current time zone after creation.
- `WeekID` represents a fixed Monday-through-Sunday week.
- Daily, Weekly, and Custom Goal frequencies enforce the approved target rules; Custom permits 2 through 6.
- Clock, Gregorian calendar, time zone, and UUID generation are injectable.
- Domain values are immutable and Sendable and do not import SwiftData.
- Tests cover Sunday-to-Monday rollover, month and year boundaries, daylight-saving transitions, and time-zone changes.

## Clarification notes

This slice extracts the approved date and frequency foundation from F-002 without changing Decision 003 or product behavior.
