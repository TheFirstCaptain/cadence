# Feature F-005B: Month History

## Objective

Add a calendar Month view that presents full Monday-through-Sunday Goal rows, calendar-month iIndulged totals, prior-period navigation, and drill-down to a selected Week.

## User value

The user can notice broader patterns across a normal calendar month without distorting weekly Goal targets at month boundaries.

## Non-goals

- Rolling 30-day periods.
- Splitting weekly Goal targets at calendar-month boundaries.
- Day detail or historical correction.
- Charts, percentages, comparisons, forecasts, or combined scores.

## Acceptance notes

- The Week/Month control switches between explicit Week and Month period identity using the clarified cross-month mapping rule.
- Month means one Gregorian calendar month in the captured viewing time zone.
- Prior months are available without an artificial limit; future months are unavailable; Today returns to the current month.
- One atomic repository snapshot returns every complete Monday-through-Sunday week overlapping the selected month plus occurrences whose preserved local day lies inside that month.
- Goal presentation uses compact full-week rows, including days outside the selected month, so weekly targets remain intact.
- Calendar-month iIndulged totals exclude occurrences whose preserved local day falls outside the selected month.
- Every category required by the F-005A inclusion contract remains visible, including zero counts.
- Tapping a week changes to its Week view without losing coherent period navigation.
- A narrow composition-owned History revision can request a fresh atomic Month read after a later correction without coupling feature models or exposing persistence details.
- Historical records are never regrouped after time-zone travel.

## Clarification notes

- F-005A owns shared History navigation, screen-state, category-inclusion, and neutral presentation contracts.
- Month aggregation must be atomic rather than assembled from independent Week/day reads that could observe different repository states.
