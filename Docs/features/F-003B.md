# Feature F-003B: My Day Goal Checkoffs

## Objective

Add immediate, reversible one-tap Goal completion to My Day while preserving Daily, Weekly, and Custom progress rules and historical integrity.

## User value

The user can record or correct today's intended actions in one tap without Goals disappearing, judgment, or streak pressure.

## Non-goals

- Creating, editing, removing, or reordering Goals.
- Recording iIndulged occurrences.
- Correcting checkoffs on past dates.
- Adding scores, percentages, streaks, sounds, banners, or celebration effects.

## Acceptance notes

- Tapping an incomplete Goal records today's checkoff and updates the row immediately.
- Tapping a completed Goal removes today's checkoff and restores the prior state.
- Each Goal accepts at most one checkoff per local day.
- Daily Goals remain visible after completion.
- Weekly Goals remain visible as 1 of 1 through Sunday after completion.
- Custom Goals show completed-of-target progress, remain available on later days until the target is met, and remain visible afterward.
- Failed mutations restore coherent presentation state and show calm, actionable guidance without exposing storage errors.
- Relaunch restores the persisted state.
- Controls expose clear VoiceOver state and actions, use quiet feedback, and avoid nonessential motion.

## Clarification notes

This slice implements the approved binary Goal interaction. One checkoff represents completion for one day, never a quantity or repeated count.
