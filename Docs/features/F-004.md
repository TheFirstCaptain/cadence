# Feature F-004: My Goals

## Objective

Provide a My Goals tab where the user creates and manages Goals for an upcoming Monday-through-Sunday week using Daily, Weekly, or Custom frequency.

## User value

The user can set a realistic weekly intention once, carry it forward with little effort, and adapt it when life changes without losing prior accomplishments or being penalized.

## Non-goals

- Finalizing how Weekly and Custom Goals appear or behave on My Day.
- Adding selected-weekday scheduling, multiple completions per day, quantities, duration, or units.
- Adding reminders or notifications.
- Designing History beyond preserving prior checkoffs.
- Creating the Xcode project before F-002 is approved.

## Acceptance notes

- The working tab name is My Goals.
- A Goal requires a short text name and one frequency.
- Daily means once each day of the Monday-through-Sunday week.
- Weekly means once at any point during the week.
- Custom allows a target from 2 through 6 days during the week.
- A Goal can receive at most one checkoff per day; Custom progress counts distinct completed days.
- Sunday Planning edits the upcoming Monday-through-Sunday plan while the current Sunday remains part of the ending week.
- Goals carry forward automatically when the user does not complete Sunday Planning.
- The user may add, rename, change the frequency of, or remove a Goal during an active week.
- A midweek add or frequency change applies immediately without prorating the weekly target.
- Existing checkoffs count toward a changed frequency.
- Removing a Goal midweek removes it from remaining My Day views while preserving earlier checkoffs in History.
- Exact duplicate Goal names are prevented within the same weekly plan.
- My Goals may gently encourage a manageable list but does not impose a hard Goal limit.

## Clarification notes

- The user selected My Goals as the working name.
- Weekly completion may happen at any point during the week.
- Custom is intentionally limited to 2–6 because one time is Weekly and seven times is Daily.
- The planning week is fixed as Monday through Sunday; Sunday is the planning day for the upcoming week and the last day of the active week.
- Goals automatically carry into the next week.
- Active-week editing is allowed.
- Exact duplicate names in one plan should be prevented.
- A gentle manageable-list suggestion is preferred over a hard maximum.
- The user approved immediate, non-prorated midweek changes and preservation of historical checkoffs.
