# Feature F-003: My Day

## Objective

Make My Day the default Cadence destination, combining today's binary Goal checkoffs with fast, timestamped iCheated occurrence tracking.

## User value

The user can understand and record the day in one place: first what they intended to do, then indulgences they want to notice without judgment.

## Non-goals

- Defining the My Goals workflow or frequency rules.
- Adding custom iCheated categories in this feature.
- Designing History beyond retaining records for it.
- Adding quantities, servings, units, scores, or punitive streaks.
- Creating the Xcode project before F-002 is approved.

## Acceptance notes

- Cadence opens to My Day.
- Goals appears before iCheated.
- Goals supplied by My Goals for the current day are visible.
- Each Goal is complete or incomplete, remains visible after completion, and can be toggled in one tap.
- iCheated initially includes, in order, Junk Food, Unhealthy Snacks, Candy, Alcohol, and Desserts.
- Each category row shows an icon, type, minus control, today's count, and plus control.
- One plus tap records one occurrence with its date and time and increases today's count by one.
- One minus tap removes the most recently recorded occurrence for that category today and decreases the count by one.
- Counts cannot fall below zero.
- Today follows the device's local calendar.
- Prior occurrences remain retained for History unless removed through the correction behavior.
- iCheated remains the user-facing name and should feel playful rather than judgmental.

## Clarification notes

- The user chose Goals before iCheated.
- Goal tracking is complete or incomplete, not a daily count.
- Completed Goals remain visible and can be unchecked.
- One iCheated tap means one occurrence, not a serving or measured quantity.
- The minus control is the immediate correction mechanism.
- Additional iCheated categories may be added later but are outside the clarified V1 behavior.
- The device's local calendar determines the current day.

## Validation plan

- Unit-test Goal toggle behavior and idempotent correction.
- Unit-test plus/minus persistence, most-recent removal, zero floor, and retained timestamps.
- Test local midnight, Sunday boundaries, daylight-saving transitions, and time-zone changes.
- Verify Goals remain above iCheated and completed Goals remain visible.
- Verify initial category order, icons, VoiceOver labels, Dynamic Type, and one-handed operation.
- Verify relaunch restores today's state and prior records remain available for History.
- Review all copy for a playful, nonjudgmental tone.
